# Visual Style Bible — *The Keeper of Cape Vespers*

## Overall style

Cinematic, painterly, cold-warm. The look is a cross between an Andrew Wyeth tempera painting and a contemporary cold-climate drama film (*The Lighthouse* without the grotesque; *The Vanishing* without the melodrama; *Lean on Pete* with more fog).

**Forbidden:** teal-and-orange color grading; hyperreal plastic skin; flat cartoon style; anime/manga; AI-typical soft-glow portrait; text or watermarks.

## Aspect ratio, resolution, frame

- All scene images: 16:9 at 1344x768 (z-ai image supports this exact size).
- Thumbnail: 1280x720 (YouTube 16:9).
- Render: 1920x1080, 30 fps, h264, AAC audio, mp4.
- Film-grain overlay: very light, applied uniformly in post (not per-image).

## Color palette

- **Sky/water:** cool blue-grey (#5A6878 to #8FA2B8).
- **Lamp light:** warm amber (#F2B265, #E89B3D, #C97A2A).
- **Wood:** salt-bleached grey (#C9C2B0), weathered brown (#5C4A38).
- **Fir/cottage roof:** deep green (#2A4236).
- **Costumes:**
  - Iris's navy coat: #2A3A52 with salt-faded sheen.
  - Iris's red knit cap: #B5483A (muted brick red, not fire-engine).
  - Halen's yellow oilskin: #D4A634 (mustard-yellow, sun-faded).
  - Wren's service windbreaker: #34425B (navy grey).
  - Renner's slicker: #D8B547 (yellowed oilskin, like an old fisherman's).
- **Forbidden:** pure saturated colors; the palette must always feel sun-bleach or fog-muted.

## Lighting logic

- Dawn / dusk: cold blue-grey, soft amber from the lamp.
- Noon: clear warm sun, hard shadows.
- Night: dark blue-grey, never black; amber lamp rotation sweeping across the scene every 15 seconds (a visual motif of the rotating lens).
- Fog: cool blue-grey; never orange.
- Wood interiors: warm amber from gas lamp or wood stove.
- **Lighting rule:** if the lamp is visible in a shot at night, it must be sweeping; the sweep is the film's heartbeat.

## Composition

- Wide shots for geography and arrival.
- Medium shots for character interaction.
- Close-ups on hands, eyes, key props (the notebook, the crank handle, the contact sheet).
- Slow push-ins for tension; static for grief; slow pull-outs for transitions.
- One hard cut for the midpoint reveal (S14 SH02 → S14 SH03).

## Lens language

- Wide shots: 24mm equivalent, deep depth of field.
- Medium shots: 35mm equivalent.
- Close-ups: 50mm equivalent, shallow depth of field.
- Patient motion: very slow push-in (over 6s), slow tilt-up, slow pull-out. No whip pans, no handheld shake.

## Visual motifs (must recur)

1. **The rotating lens sweep** — the amber lamp rotation passes across the frame every 15 seconds. This is the visual heartbeat of the film.
2. **Iris's aunt's coat** — too big in the shoulders; she wears it from scene 3 onward.
3. **The red knit cap** — Iris's second continuity anchor, from scene 6 onward.
4. **The green notebook** — small, green cover, ruled pages, in code.
5. **The brass crank handle** — the climactic prop.
6. **The fog** — closes the world, then opens it again at the end.
7. **The dock** — the single exit; always one boat at it, or none.

## Forbidden visual elements

- No text or watermarks in any image.
- No real actors' faces.
- No depictions of the Visitor's full face until scene 20 (climax). Before that, he is a silhouette or a profile in a small photograph.
- No depiction of Wren's arrest — happens off-screen, heard on shortwave.
- No melodramatic weather (no lightning strikes; no rainbows).
- No cell phones, no modern cars (the island has no roads; no cell service).
- No teal-and-orange color grading.
- No green-screen artifacts.
- No anime/manga style.
- No hyperreal plastic skin; skin must look like skin.

## Continuity anchor rules (must obey in every image)

- Iris: black hair tied back with a leather cord; small scar on right eyebrow; small lighthouse tattoo inside left wrist (visible if her wrist is in frame); slim, small-boned, calloused hands. Costume progression per continuity_rules.md.
- Halen: very short salt-bleached hair; yellow oilskin jacket; navy knit watchman's cap.
- Wren: blonde-grey jaw-length bob; navy service windbreaker with patch on left chest; clipboard.
- Renner: tall, broad-shouldered, rolling walk; yellow fisherman's rain slicker; dark knit cap; grey hair visible.
- The lighthouse: white tower with one black band in the middle; lens rotation clockwise (viewed from above).
- The cottage: weathered grey shingles, white window trim, green roof.

## Style anchor text (appended to every image prompt)

> Cinematic 16:9, painterly, slight film grain, cold blue-grey Pacific Northwest palette with warm amber lamp accents. Slight vignette. No text, no watermarks. Andrew Wyeth tempera feel crossed with cold-climate drama film. Photoreal but not plastic; skin has texture; wood has grain.
