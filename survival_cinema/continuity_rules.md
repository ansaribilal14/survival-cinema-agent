## 2. Costume progression (Iris)
- Scenes 1–4: olive field jacket, grey wool sweater, dark jeans, brown boots.
- Scenes 5–22: aunt's navy lighthouse-keeper coat (too big in shoulders) over grey sweater; red knit cap from scene 8 onward.
- Climax (20–21): navy coat over pyjamas, red cap, no gloves.
- After (22): same.
- Coda: not Iris — Iris's photograph shows the lighthouse.

The navy coat and red cap are the continuity anchors for Iris across scenes 5–22.

## 3. Injury progression (Iris)
- Scenes 1–7: clean, well-rested.
- Scenes 8–15: tired (dark circles), still clean.
- Scenes 16–19: tired, small cut on right index finger from prying the boathouse frame.
- Scenes 20–21: soaked through, red cap low on forehead, hair wet, the small cut scabbed.
- Scene 22: dry clothes (changed), exhausted but upright.

## 4. Character appearance anchors

### Iris
- Hair: black, straight, shoulder-length, tied back with a leather cord. NEVER loose (except S05 just-woken).
- Eye color: dark brown. Skin: light brown, freckles on nose.
- Small scar: right eyebrow. Tattoo: small lighthouse silhouette, inside left wrist.
- Body type: slim, small-boned, calloused hands.

### Marisol
- Hair: black streaked grey, in a braid. Skin: weathered, lighter brown than Iris.
- Anchor tattoo: left forearm. Body type: stockier than Iris.

### Halen
- Hair: very short, salt-bleached almost white. Skin: pale, freckled.
- Always in yellow oilskin jacket. Cap: navy knit watchman's cap.

### Wren
- Hair: blonde-grey, jaw-length bob. Skin: fair.
- Navy lighthouse-service windbreaker with patch on left chest. Clipboard in right hand.

### Renner
- Hair: grey, thinning, under a dark knit cap. Skin: sun-weathered, sun-cancer red.
- Yellow fisherman's rain slicker over heavy grey sweater. Tall, broad shoulders, rolling walk.

## 5. Prop continuity

### The green notebook
- Small, green cover, ruled pages. Marisol's handwriting in code (mixed letters and numbers).
- Appears in scenes 7, 8, 11, 16, 22 (in a waterproof bag).

### The metal box
- Small, water-tight, olive-green metal box (military ammo-box style).
- Hidden under the floorboard under the kitchen stove.
- Appears in scenes 7, 11, 22.

### The contact sheet / negatives
- A strip of four frames; one frame shows Renner in profile at the dock.
- The print: 8x10 black-and-white, taken with the Yashica medium-format.
- Appears in scenes 11, 12, 14, 22.

### The crank handle
- Brass, ~30 cm long, kept in the lamp room closet.
- Engages both the lens rotation cam and the fog-signal whistle cam.
- Appears in scenes 19 (foreshadow), 20, 21 (climax).

### The navy wool coat
- Marisol's, too big in the shoulders for Iris. Iris's visual continuity anchor from scene 3 onward.

### The red knit cap
- Iris's second visual continuity anchor from scene 8 onward.

### The camera
- Marisol's old Yashica medium-format with a leather strap. Iris carries it on the bow in scene 1 and uses it in scenes 6, 10, 12, 17.

## 6. Geographic continuity

### View from the lamp room catwalk
- Looking north: open water, Olympic Mountains on the horizon to the east.
- Looking south: the dock, the path, the cottage, the fir grove, the small graveyard.
- Looking east: the open Pacific beyond the strait.
- Looking west: Vancouver Island silhouette in clear weather.

### View from the dock
- Looking back at the island: white-and-black lighthouse, green cottage, green roof, the fir grove to the south.

### View from the path
- Looking toward the dock: salt-bleached wood, the boathouse to the right, the water beyond.
- Looking toward the cottage: the path goes up a slight rise; the cottage sits on the rise with the tower behind it.

## 7. Lighting and color rules
- Lamp light: warm amber (~3000K).
- Daylight: cool blue-grey (~5600K) except when explicitly "warm noon light."
- Fog: cool blue-grey; never orange.
- Wood interiors: warm amber from wood stove / gas lamp.
- Night exteriors: dark blue-grey, never black.
- Avoid teal-and-orange color grading.

## 8. Forbidden deviations
- No cell phones visible on screen (no cell service on the island).
- No modern cars visible (the island has no roads).
- No green-screen artifacts. No text or watermarks in images.
- No use of real actors' faces as references.
- No depiction of the Visitor's face until scene 20.
- No depiction of Wren's arrest — happens off-screen, heard on shortwave.
- No melodramatic orchestral music. One piano theme, three chords, used twice.

## 9. Originality commitments
No scene, location, character, dialogue, or plot element is borrowed from any reference work. The lighthouse setting does not appear in any reference. The smuggling plot does not appear in any reference. The fog-signal-whistle climax does not appear in any reference. The female inspector antagonist has no analog in any reference.
