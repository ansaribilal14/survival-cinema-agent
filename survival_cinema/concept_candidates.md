# Concept Candidates — Original Female-Centric YA Survival Thriller

## Concept 1 — "The Last Wildfire Lookout"

A young woman, Sena, age 22, takes a summer job as a fire-lookout in a remote Montana tower after the death of her mother. Three weeks in, she sees smoke from a fire that the radio cannot confirm. She realizes a fire has been *set* and is being *allowed* to grow by someone inside the agency.

**Pros:** High agency, very cinematic, fire weather well documented.
**Cons:** The "talking to a recording" twist may stretch credulity; smoke/fire hard to render consistently in AI images.

## Concept 2 — "The Tide Line"

A young marine-biology PhD student, Mira, age 24, is the only paying guest at a remote tide-pool research inn on the Oregon coast. The other guests are three older scientists running a "longitudinal study of an invasive sea slug." The cook warns Mira in code that the "study" is something else — a pathogen breeding operation.

**Pros:** Tide-pool bio-thriller is fresh; strong female supporting cast.
**Cons:** Pathogen reveal may feel like a genre cliché; kayaks and water are difficult to render.

## Concept 3 — "The Keeper of Cape Vespers"  **[SELECTED]**

A young woman, Iris Vaughn, age 22, takes a seasonal job as the relief keeper of an automated lighthouse on a small island off the Pacific Northwest coast. The previous relief keeper — her aunt Marisol — died on the island the previous winter, ruled an accident. Three weeks in, Iris finds her aunt's journals hidden under a floorboard. They are not the journals of a woman who died accidentally. They are the journals of a woman who found something on the island she was not supposed to find, and who was being silenced by someone who came by boat every Thursday.

**Why selected:** Highest cinematic feasibility with AI image generation (lighthouse, fog, journals, wood interiors, women in coats — all strengths). Cleanest application of the Moreau lesson (moral-fact midpoint reveal), the Pym lesson (closed-arena escalation), and the Scarlet Plague lesson (frame-narrative voice from a survivor who has lost someone). Strong female supporting cast (Marisol, Halen, Wren). Antagonist logic the audience can almost follow.

**Evaluation:** Originality HIGH, Agency VERY HIGH, Survival realism HIGH, Emotional depth VERY HIGH, Suspense VERY HIGH, Cinematic opportunities VERY HIGH, Feasibility with AI images VERY HIGH, Runtime sustain GOOD.
