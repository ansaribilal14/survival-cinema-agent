## SCENE 01 — Boat at dawn
- **Scene ID:** S01 | **Chronological position:** 1 | **Duration:** 42s
- **Location:** Off Cape Vespers, on the Petrel's bow
- **Time of day:** dawn, 06:00 | **Weather:** foggy, light swell
- **Characters:** Iris Vaughn, Halen Yoo (voice)
- **Appearance:** Iris — olive field jacket, grey wool sweater, dark jeans, brown boots, hair tied back with leather cord, camera around neck, aunt's navy coat folded over her arm
- **Actions:** Iris stands on the bow as the boat approaches the island. The lighthouse appears through the fog.
- **Objective:** Establish protagonist, boat, destination.
- **Narrative purpose:** Inciting image — the protagonist returns to where her aunt died.
- **Conflict:** Internal — Iris is here to honor her aunt; Halen will tell her to go home.
- **Emotional beat:** Quiet dread; resolved determination.
- **Information revealed:** Iris is the new relief keeper; her aunt died here.
- **Information withheld:** How her aunt died; what the island hides.
- **Shots:**
  - S01_SH01 (wide establishing, static, 6s): Wide 16:9 of the lighthouse island through fog at dawn, supply boat in foreground, Iris on bow.
  - S01_SH02 (medium close-up, slow push-in, 6s): Iris's face from side, dark brown eyes, small scar on right eyebrow.
  - S01_SH03 (wide, static, 6s): The lighthouse with lamp turning, visible through thin fog.
- **Narration:** "The boat came in at dawn, the way Marisol always said it should. Iris Vaughn was twenty-two. She was the new relief keeper at Cape Vespers. Her aunt had died on this island the previous winter. Everyone said it was a fall. Iris did not believe it."
- **Dialogue:**
  - Halen: "You don't have to do this."
  - Iris: "I will think less of me."
  - Halen: "That's not a reason to be here."
- **Music:** Solo piano, three-chord theme, very soft, first appearance.
- **Ambience:** Diesel engine low, water against hull, distant gull, distant foghorn.
- **SFX:** boat diesel engine, water against hull, distant gull
- **Transition:** Slow cross-dissolve to the dock at sunrise.
- **Continuity:** Iris's hair tied back; olive field jacket; aunt's navy coat on her arm (not yet worn).

## SCENE 03 — The tower
- **Scene ID:** S03 | **Position:** 3 | **Duration:** 50s
- **Location:** Cape Vespers lighthouse tower, interior and lamp room
- **Time:** 10:00 morning | **Weather:** clear
- **Characters:** Iris
- **Actions:** Iris climbs the 112-step spiral stair. Reaches lamp room. Finds her aunt's woolen coat on a hook. Puts it on. It fits.
- **Objective:** Establish tower, lens, coat (Iris's visual anchor).
- **Narrative purpose:** Iris takes on her aunt's identity by wearing her coat.
- **Conflict:** Internal — becoming her aunt; remember or refuse to let go.
- **Emotional beat:** Tender grief; comfort of taking on a beloved's clothes.
- **Information revealed:** Lighthouse automated since 1984; original mechanism intact.
- **Information withheld:** Fog-signal whistle; bookmarked keeper's manual page.
- **Shots:**
  - S03_SH01 (low angle wide, slow tilt-up, 6s): Looking up the spiral stair, Iris ascending.
  - S03_SH02 (wide interior, static, 6s): Lamp room with Fresnel lens, Iris in doorway.
  - S03_SH03 (close-up, slow push-in, 6s): Navy wool coat on iron hook, Iris's hand lifting it.
  - S03_SH04 (medium, static, 6s): Iris in the navy coat, too big in shoulders, in lamp room.
- **Narration:** "The tower stair was a hundred and twelve steps. Iris had counted them at fourteen. She counted them again at twenty-two, the way a person touches a bruise to see if it still hurts. At the top, her aunt's woolen coat was still on the hook by the door. Iris put it on. It fit."
- **Music:** None. **Ambience:** Iron stair footsteps, wind, lens gear rotation tick.
- **Continuity:** From this scene on, Iris wears the navy wool coat. Red knit cap appears from scene 6.

## SCENE 14 — Midpoint reveal: 'the lens is the only honest witness'
- **Scene ID:** S14 | **Position:** 14 | **Duration:** 36s
- **Location:** Cape Vespers base of tower
- **Time:** 13:00 afternoon | **Weather:** clear, warm noon
- **Characters:** Iris, Wren
- **Actions:** Wren at bottom of tower says conversationally: "Your aunt always said the lens was the only honest witness on this island." Iris does not move. Line is from hidden notebook. Wren leaves on tender. Iris sits under lens.
- **Objective:** MIDPOINT REVEAL — moral fact, not monster.
- **Narrative purpose:** Iris now knows Wren is complicit. Viewer now knows.
- **Conflict:** Iris cannot show reaction. Wren confirmed she read the hidden notebook.
- **Emotional beat:** Slow recognition. Cold dread. Iris does not move.
- **Information revealed:** Wren has read the hidden notebook — therefore Wren is complicit.
- **Information withheld:** Whether Wren is the killer (no — cover-up, not killer).
- **Shots:**
  - S14_SH01 (medium two-shot, static, 6s): Wren speaking to Iris at base of tower, Iris's posture rigid.
  - S14_SH02 (extreme close-up, static, 5s): Iris's face, eyes do not blink, expression absolutely still — recognition without reaction.
  - S14_SH03 (wide, static, 6s): Wren walking away down path, Iris at base of tower, not moving.
- **Narration:** "At the bottom of the tower, Wren said, conversationally, 'Your aunt always said the lens was the only honest witness on this island.' Iris did not move. The line was from the green notebook. Iris had not told anyone she had the notebook. She smiled. She said, 'She used to say a lot of things.' Wren smiled back. Wren left on the tender. Iris sat in the lamp room with the lens turning above her and the contact sheet in her hand and understood, in a single slow breath, that her aunt had not fallen on the path. Her aunt had been killed by a man who came to the island on Thursdays, and the inspector knew."
- **Dialogue:**
  - Wren: "Your aunt always said the lens was the only honest witness on this island."
  - Iris: "She used to say a lot of things."
