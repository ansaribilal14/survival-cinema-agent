# Originality Audit — *The Keeper of Cape Vespers*

## References compared
- A. *The Narrative of Arthur Gordon Pym of Nantucket* (Poe, 1838)
- B. *The Scarlet Plague* (London, 1915)
- C. *The Island of Doctor Moreau* (Wells, 1896)

## What was extracted from the references (general principles, not content)
- From A (Pym): escalation-ladder architecture; sensory deprivation as cheap cinematic device; single-exit geography.
- From B (Scarlet Plague): frame-narrative voice from a survivor who has lost someone; "loss of one specific thing as a personal wound" device.
- From C (Moreau): midpoint reveal as a moral fact (not a monster); withholding meaning of screams; antagonist whose logic the audience can almost follow.

## Conclusion
The work "The Keeper of Cape Vespers" is an original IP. It uses broad structural techniques (escalation ladder, frame-narrative voice, midpoint-as-moral-fact) that are widely used across survival fiction and are not the distinctive property of any reference work. It does not copy any setting, character, plot, dialogue, or distinctive element from any reference.

**Verdict: PASS** — originality confirmed.
