# Input Specification — `story_idea.md`

## Required fields

```markdown
# Story Idea

**Working title:** <your title, or "TBD">
**Genre:** <e.g. "YA survival thriller">
**Protagonist:**
- Age: <20-25 for YA>
- Gender: <typically female>
- Ethnicity: <e.g. "mixed Filipina-American">
- Identity: <one-sentence character description>
- Practical skill: <e.g. "lighthouse-keeping", "marine biology fieldwork">

**Setting:** <one sentence describing a closed environment with a single exit>

**Premise:** <3-5 sentences covering:
- What the protagonist came to do
- The first wrongness she notices
- What she finds that drives the plot
- The midpoint reveal (a moral fact, not a monster)
- The climactic action she takes to save herself>

**Tone references:** <optional>
**Target runtime minutes:** <default 15>
**Forbidden content:** <optional>
**Antagonist preference:** <optional — "female antagonist with followable logic" (default)>
**Narration voice preference:** <optional — "female, controlled, expressive" (default)>
**Visual style preference:** <optional — "Pacific Northwest painterly cinematic" (default)>
```

## What makes a good idea

- **Be specific.** "A young woman investigates her aunt's death on a lighthouse island" is good.
- **Pin the geography.** Single-exit settings work best.
- **Name the midpoint reveal.** A MORAL FACT, not a monster.
- **Pin the protagonist's skill.** A specific practical skill is more cinematic than "wilderness expert."
