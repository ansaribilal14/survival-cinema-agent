# Story Idea — Template

Copy this file to `story_idea.md`, fill it in, and hand it to the Survival Cinema Agent.

```markdown
# Story Idea

**Working title:** <your title, or "TBD" to let the agent name it>
**Genre:** <e.g. "YA survival thriller", "psychological horror", "wilderness mystery", "post-apocalyptic drama">
**Protagonist:**
- Age: <20-25 for YA>
- Gender: <typically female for this system; can be any>
- Ethnicity: <e.g. "mixed Filipina-American", "Black American", "Korean-American", etc.>
- Identity: <one-sentence character description>
- Practical skill: <e.g. "lighthouse-keeping", "marine biology fieldwork", "fire-lookout observation", "tide-pool research", "sound engineering", "navigation by compass">

**Setting:** <one sentence describing the closed environment with a single exit. e.g. "An automated lighthouse on a small island in the Salish Sea, accessible only by a single weekly supply boat.">

**Premise:** <3-5 sentences describing the central situation. Include:>
- What the protagonist came to do.
- The first wrongness she notices.
- What she finds that drives the plot (a journal, a recording, a photograph, a witness).
- The midpoint reveal (a moral fact, not a monster).
- The climactic action she takes to save herself.

**Tone references:** <optional — films/books/styles to evoke. e.g. "The Lighthouse (2019) without the grotesque; The Vanishing; Lean on Pete with more fog".>

**Target runtime minutes:** <default 15; range 10-25>

**Forbidden content:** <optional — anything you do not want depicted. e.g. "no graphic violence; no sexual assault references; no real-actor likenesses; no cell phones visible on screen".>

**Antagonist preference:** <optional — "female antagonist with followable logic" (default), "environmental antagonist only", "unclear/mysterious antagonist revealed at climax">

**Narration voice preference:** <optional — "female, controlled, expressive" (default), "male, deep, documentary", "neutral, audiobook-style">

**Visual style preference:** <optional — "Pacific Northwest painterly cinematic" (default), "Scandinavian cold minimalism", "Appalachian gothic", "desert brutalist">
```

## Defaults the agent fills

If any field is missing, the agent picks reasonable defaults:
- Working title → agent generates three candidates, picks the strongest
- Genre → "YA survival thriller"
- Protagonist age → 22
- Protagonist gender → female
- Tone references → genre defaults
- Runtime → 15 minutes
- Antagonist → female antagonist with followable logic
- Narration voice → female, controlled, expressive
- Visual style → Pacific Northwest painterly cinematic with cold blue-grey palette

## What makes a good story idea

**Be specific.** "A young woman investigates her aunt's death on a lighthouse island" is good. "A survival story" is too vague.

**Pin the geography.** Single-exit settings (an island, a tower, a station) work best. Open-ended environments diffuse the suspense.

**Name the midpoint reveal.** The midpoint reveal should be a MORAL FACT (the inspector is complicit; the host is not what they seem), not a monster reveal. State the fact in your premise.

**Pin the protagonist's skill.** A specific practical skill (lighthouse-keeping, marine biology, fire-lookout observation, sound engineering, navigation) is more cinematic than "wilderness expert."
